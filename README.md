I build gameplay systems in **Unreal Engine 5**: combat, abilities, interaction, movement and AI. Most of my work is in **Blueprint**, and I'm actively moving systems into **C++** for cleaner gameplay architecture. I take projects from concept to playable quickly, often under game-jam deadlines, and I enjoy the debugging and tuning that makes a mechanic feel right.

- 🕷️ Currently building **[SpiderLast](https://github.com/RandomDeVoloper/SpiderLast)**, a web-swing and wall-climbing parkour game (solo, UE 5.8)
- 🌱 Currently learning: Blueprint → C++ translation, profiling and optimization (frame budget, memory, draw calls)
- 🎓 Digital Game Design (B.A.), İstanbul Esenyurt University, 2023 – 2027 · Unreal Engine Certification (Navras Akademi)
